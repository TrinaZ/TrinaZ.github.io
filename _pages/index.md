# Tian Zheng
Ph.D. Student at Xi'an Jiaotong University

My research focuses on **bioinformatics, machine learning, and computational genomics**. I am particularly interested in developing deep learning and transfer learning methods for analyzing cancer sequencing data, including mutation detection, tumor purity estimation, and structural variation genotyping.

## Research Interests
- Genomic variant detection (SNV, indel, structural variation)
- Transfer learning for subclonal proportion estimation
- High-performance computing for bioinformatics (AlphaFold optimization)
- TCR epitope binding prediction
